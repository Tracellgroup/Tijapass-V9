# TijaPass V8 — Demo Guide for Bank of Tanzania / Developers

The pilot is a **concept and workflow demonstration**, not a licensed payment service or approved lending application. Do not use real identities, account statements or actual funds.

**Commercial value story:** TijaPass helps an MSME record sales/expenses and see clearer business figures. Verified history can, with user consent, support a financing institution's credit review. Financial institutions may contract with TFL for qualified information access and services. Customer basic record keeping should stay free.

1. Open Customer dashboard, Kiswahili: `Habari`, `Rekodi mauzo`, `Rekodi matumizi`, `Taarifa na faida`, `Uliza TijaPass`.
2. Show record a sale, mandatory description, duplicate and unusual-amount check, accountant review and journal reports from V7.
3. Open TijaPass Msaidizi: demonstrate FAQ help and human escalation, then switch role to TFL to reply in the browser demo.
4. Show transparent plan choices (Basic free / Weekly TZS 1,000 / Plus TZS 3,000 / Pro TZS 8,000 / day pass TZS 500). Clarify no payment service or paid subscriber is live.
5. Show fi/supplier/TFL roles, permissions concept, loan offer, funding wallet and business records; distinguish screen simulation from secure server operations.
6. Request regulatory guidance on live testing, personal-data controls, licensed payment partners, consumer protection, FI fees and handling consented financial information.

**Explicit limits:** offline predefined help (not deployed AI chatbot), localStorage data (not secure multi-device DB), no connected bank/MNO, no actual cash, customer onboarding and statements not independently reconciled, no automated billing.
